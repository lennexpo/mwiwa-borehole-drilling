Who We Are

Harare's Fully Equipped Borehole Company

Mwiwa Borehole Drilling is based in Kamfinsa, Harare, Zimbabwe. We are a fully equipped, professional borehole drilling company — which means we own and operate our own heavy drilling machinery on every single project.

That distinction matters. No delays waiting for a subcontractor's rig. No inflated costs from third-party hire. No gaps in accountability. Just our team, our equipment, and a direct commitment to delivering water to your property.

From the initial geophysical site survey through to full pump installation and commissioning, we handle every stage in-house. Over 200 projects delivered across residential, commercial, agricultural, and industrial sectors throughout Zimbabwe.

Commercial

The Process

From First Call to Running Water

A straightforward end-to-end process, managed entirely by our team.

01

Consultation & Quote

Contact us by phone or WhatsApp. We discuss your property, intended use, and site conditions — then provide a clear quote with no hidden extras.

02

Geophysical Site Survey

We use survey equipment to locate the optimal drilling point, maximising water yield before a single metre of ground is broken.

03

Drilling & Casing

Our heavy rig drills to the target aquifer. Casing, screens, and headworks are installed to approved standards for lasting performance.

04

Pump Installation

Submersible or surface pump — supplied, installed, wired, and tested. We also design and install solar-powered pump systems for off-grid sites.

05

Water Testing

Pump yield and water quality are tested and documented at the end of every project so you have a complete record of your borehole's performance.

06

Handover & Support

We walk you through the system and remain available for any follow-up questions. Your water is running — and we stand behind the work.
